# Aura Affinity Map

> **Status: thematic business and map directory.** Inclusion does not assert endorsement, affiliation, accuracy or current availability. Check every business directly before relying on its listing or contact details.

Aura Affinity is a browser map and directory that gathers public business listings around themes such as Aura, chakra, aurora, yoga and tai chi. It is an exploratory way to browse thematic name and category matches, rather than a verified network of connected organisations.

Visit the [public map](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-affinity/) or inspect the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-affinity).

## What is in the directory

The page displays a CSV-backed map, filters and listing cards. Its entries may include public business names, locations, websites, phone numbers, social links and short descriptions. Those details can change, and the presence of a listing does not mean the business has approved, endorsed or joined Aura Affinity.

## Find your way around

- [index.html](index.html) contains the interactive map, filters and downloadable directory view.
- [aura-affinity.csv](aura-affinity.csv) is the current listing data used by the page.
- [run-map-update.py](run-map-update.py) and [curation](curation) contain the update and curation inputs; [the workflow](.github/workflows/update-map-data.yml) runs the mapped data update.
- [LICENSE](LICENSE) sets the public-source terms for this repository.
